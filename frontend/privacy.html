Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you search for a location or coordinates on this platform, your search query is transmitted to our geocoding service (OpenStreetMap Nominatim) to retrieve geographic coordinates. We do not require user accounts, and we do not store personal profiles or search histories on our servers.

2. Third-Party Map Tile Providers

Map imagery and raster layers are requested directly by your browser from Esri ArcGIS Online and Google Earth Engine. These providers may receive your IP address and standard HTTP request headers as part of serving map tiles.

3. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies, behavioral trackers, or third-party analytics scripts.

4. Contact

For questions regarding data handling, contact the site administrator through your registered domain contact address prior to public deployment.
