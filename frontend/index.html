    NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) Measures live green vegetation density using Red and Near-Infrared light: (NIR - Red) / (NIR + Red), ranging from -1.0 to +1.0.
    Usefulness: Tracks crop health, detects drought stress early, separates water bodies (< 0.0) and built-up urban zones (0.0 to 0.2) from active cropland and forest canopy (> 0.4).
    EVI (Enhanced Vegetation Index) Optimizes the vegetation signal in high-biomass regions by correcting for soil background noise and atmospheric aerosol scattering.
    Usefulness: Prevents signal saturation over dense multi-layer crop canopies and tropical forests where standard NDVI plateaus.
